# Gratuity Nomination — Form F

## Overview
The Stemz Gratuity Nomination Policy requires every employee to complete Form F under Rule 5(1) of the Payment of Gratuity Act 1972 at joining. This form nominates the family member(s) who will receive the employee's gratuity payment if it becomes due after the employee's death, or is owing at the time of death. Nominees must be family members within the meaning of the Act. The form captures nominee names and addresses, relationships, ages, and the proportion of gratuity each will receive, together with the employee's personal details (name, department, date of appointment, permanent address). A new Form F invalidates any previous nomination. The nomination must be signed in the presence of witnesses and certified by the employer.

## Purpose of the Form
Every employee at Stemz must complete Form F under Rule 5(1) of the Payment of Gratuity Act 1972 at joining. The form nominates the person or persons who will receive the gratuity payable after the employee's death, or the gratuity standing to the employee's credit if it becomes payable but has not been paid before the employee's death.

## Who Can Be Nominated
Nominees must be members of the employee's family within the meaning of clause (h) of section 2 of the Payment of Gratuity Act 1972. The employee certifies in the form that the nominated person or persons meet this requirement.

## Nomination Details Required
For each nominee, the employee provides the nominee's full name and address, relationship to the employee, age, and the proportion of gratuity that will be shared with that nominee.

## Employee Statement Required
The form requires the employee's full name, sex, religion, marital status (unmarried, married, widow, or widower), department or branch, post held with ticket or serial number if applicable, date of appointment, and permanent address.

## Declaration Options
The employee can declare that they have no family within the meaning of the Act, or that their father/mother/parents are not dependent on them, or that they have excluded certain members from their family by prior notice to the controlling authority.

## Nomination Overrides Previous Nomination
A new nomination made through Form F invalidates any previous nomination by the same employee.

## Witnesses and Employer Certification
The nomination must be signed in the presence of witnesses, whose full names and addresses are recorded on the form. The employer then certifies that the nomination has been verified and recorded.

## Employee Retains a Copy
The employee receives a duplicate copy of the nomination in Form F, filled by them and duly certified by the employer, and acknowledges receipt on the form.
