# Data Protection and Privacy Undertaking

## Overview
The Stemz Data Protection and Privacy Undertaking governs how employees handle confidential company data and visa applicant information. It covers the types of data employees encounter (applicant PII, company reports and presentations, internal communications), prohibits unauthorised sharing (including to personal email, family, friends), prohibits external disclosure, requires due care in handling, restricts use to work-assigned purposes only, mandates compliance with Stemz Information Security and Data Privacy policies (zero-tolerance policy), governs use of company resources (laptops, storage, internet, email), applies for the duration of employment and afterwards, and grants Stemz the right to monitor resource usage for compliance.

## Types of Data Handled
During employment with Stemz Healthcare, employees handle confidential data belonging to visa applicants (such as name, passport number, postal address, email address, and phone number). Employees also handle company confidential and internal data, including reports, presentations, emails with critical company and customer information, internal communication notes, and awareness and training materials.

## No Unauthorised Sharing of Company Data
Employees must not pass on any company confidential or internal information, visa applicant information, or business secrets to anyone not authorised to receive such information. This restriction includes personal email IDs, family members, and friends. Confidential information must never be used for any unauthorised, unethical, or inappropriate purpose.

## No External Disclosure
Employees must not pass on or make use of confidential or internal information outside of Stemz Group to any person, firm, or business in an unauthorised manner. This applies whether the information is explicitly marked confidential or not, and includes physical, electronic, and verbal disclosure.

## Due Care in Handling
Employees must exercise due care in protecting all confidential and internal company information.

## Restricted Use
Employees may use confidential or internal information only in connection with the work assigned to them by Stemz Healthcare and its authorised representatives. Any other use is prohibited.

## Compliance with Information Security and Data Privacy Policies
Employees must read, understand, and comply with the Stemz Healthcare Information Security and Data Privacy policies. Stemz Healthcare enforces a zero-tolerance policy on non-compliance with these policies.

## Use of Company Resources
Stemz may provide resources such as laptops, desktops, electronic devices, internet access, email access, storage media, USB storage privileges, and CD writing access. Employees must use these resources and privileges with due care and responsibility, in compliance with Stemz Information Security and Data Privacy policies. These resources must never be used to pass information outside of Stemz.

## Continued Obligation
This undertaking continues to apply at all times in the future, and survives completion of the work assigned to the employee or cessation of employment with Stemz for any reason.

## Right to Monitor
The employee permits Stemz Healthcare Group to monitor the usage of resources and assets provided to them, to detect non-compliance with Information Security and Data Privacy policies. The employee agrees that Stemz has the right to take disciplinary or other action if the employee is found in violation of Stemz policies or the terms of this undertaking.
