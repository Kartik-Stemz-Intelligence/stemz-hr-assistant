# Undertaking-cum-Indemnity Agreement — Signed at Exit

## Overview
The Stemz Undertaking-cum-Indemnity Agreement is signed by every separating employee at exit before exit clearance is approved. It affirms the employee's understanding of the confidentiality clauses from their appointment letter. It covers: intellectual property (all IP developed at Stemz is deemed "work on hire" and belongs to Stemz), a 5-year post-employment confidentiality obligation for company confidential information, prohibition on defaming the company on social media, non-solicitation of Stemz employees, liquidated damages of 6 times last drawn monthly salary for breach, exclusive New Delhi jurisdiction under Indian law, voluntary execution by the employee, and signature witnessed by two witnesses.

## Purpose
This undertaking is signed by every separating employee at exit as part of the exit process. It affirms the employee's understanding of confidentiality clauses from their appointment letter and must be signed before exit clearance is approved.

## Intellectual Property Ownership
Any intellectual property developed by the employee while employed with Stemz is deemed to be "work on hire". The proprietary rights on such intellectual property always lie with Stemz.

## 5-Year Confidentiality Obligation
For a period of 5 years from the date of this undertaking, the departing employee must not divulge — in writing or otherwise — any Confidential Information, data, knowledge, or client names relating to the conduct and management of the business of Stemz (or its associated entities). This restriction applies to disclosure to any person or entity, including any competitor of the company or its associated entities and subsidiaries. Disclosure is prohibited if it has the effect of causing financial loss or impairing the business interests of the company.

## Definition of Confidential Information
Confidential Information means any and all non-public information disclosed by the company that should reasonably be understood as confidential in the context of its disclosure (whether or not designated as "Confidential" or "Proprietary"). This includes but is not limited to: sales and operating information, existing and potential business and marketing plans, strategies, financial information, cost and pricing information, data media, know-how, designs, drawings, specifications, source codes, technical information, client information, concepts, reports, methods, processes, techniques, operations, devices, internal processes and policies, disciplinary meetings, and ongoing investigations.

## No Defamation on Social Media
The departing employee undertakes not to upload, write, or spread articles on social media, messaging applications (such as WhatsApp), or other online tools with reviews that have the tendency of defaming the reputation, goodwill, or clean and transparent image of the company. This restriction applies to public comments, professional media, and anonymous posts that can be traced back to the employee.

## Non-Solicitation of Employees
The departing employee undertakes not to seek or make an employment offer — as employee or consultant, on their own behalf or on behalf of any other person or company — to any employee working within Stemz or within an entity belonging to the Stemz group.

## Liquidated Damages for Breach
If the departing employee is found in breach of this undertaking and indulges in activity that harms the reputation and goodwill of the company, they must pay liquidated damages equal to 6 (six) times their last drawn monthly salary to the company. This applies whether or not the company suffers or sustains any financial loss due to such acts and misconduct. The liquidated damages are without prejudice to any other rights available to the company, including obtaining injunctive relief through the courts.

## Governing Law and Jurisdiction
The validity and interpretation of this agreement, and the legal relations of the parties to it, are governed by the laws of India. Disputes are subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Courts in New Delhi, India.

## Voluntary Execution
The departing employee signs and executes this Undertaking-cum-Indemnity Agreement voluntarily, willingly, and without any pressure, coercion, or undue influence.

## Witness Requirement
The undertaking must be signed in the presence of two witnesses. Each witness records their name, address, date, ID type and number, and signature on the undertaking.
