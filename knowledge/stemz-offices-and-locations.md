# Stemz Offices and Locations

## Corporate Headquarters (Netherlands)
Stemz Global is headquartered in the Netherlands. The corporate HQ is the parent-entity office for the multinational healthcare group.

## Gurugram — Corporate Office (India)
The Stemz corporate office in Gurugram is at 1st Floor, Tower-B, Emaar Digital Greens, Golf Course Extension Road, Sector 61, Gurugram, Haryana - 122011. This is the address listed on the Stemz website as the primary India contact.

## Gurugram — Head Office for HR / Day 1 Reporting
New joiners at Gurugram report to the Head Office (HO) at Plot No. 31, Udyog Vihar, Phase 1, Gurugram, Haryana - 122022 on their first day. This is the reporting location per the Welcome Email issued to new joiners. If in doubt about which Gurugram location to visit as a new joiner, confirm with the HR SPOC on the Welcome Email.

## Dubai Office (United Arab Emirates)
The Dubai office is at HDS Business Centre, Office 2001, 20th Floor, Cluster M, Jumeirah Lakes Towers, Dubai, UAE.

## Other Career Locations
Stemz recruits candidates across multiple offices in Asia. Careers are open at the following locations: Chennai (India), Mumbai (India), Kochi (India), Delhi (India), Hyderabad (India), Lucknow (India), Kolkata (India), Gurugram (India), Colombo (Sri Lanka), and Quezon City (Philippines).

## Visa Medical Centres
Stemz operates over 15 medical centres across 7 countries in South and Southeast Asia, including Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Bangladesh, Nepal, and the Philippines. These are operational service centres for immigration health examinations.

## QMC — Second HR Rollout Location
Beyond the Head Office (HO) rollout of the HR automation pilot, Stemz plans a gradual rollout to QMC as the second location. QMC refers to a Stemz operational campus that is part of the internal HR programme rollout plan. Employees at QMC should refer to their local HR SPOC for location-specific onboarding details.

## Primary Contact Channels
The primary corporate contact email is info@stemzglobal.com. Direct phone numbers for offices or departments are not published on the Stemz website; instead, use the website contact form and select the request type (Information Request, Proposal Request, Partnership or Alliance, Investor Relations, Media Relations, or Job Enquiries).

## Social Media
Stemz maintains active presence on LinkedIn (https://www.linkedin.com/company/stemzhealthcare/), Facebook, and Instagram (@StemzGlobal and @Stemzhealthcare).
