# Laptop Usage and Employee Acknowledgement

## Overview
The Stemz Laptop Usage and Acknowledgement Policy governs how employees are issued and must handle company laptops. Every employee issued a company laptop signs a Laptop Acknowledgement Form and agrees to the rules covered by this policy: sole responsibility for upkeep and custody (with financial liability for negligence-caused damage or loss), use only in accordance with company policy including the Acceptable Use of Internet section, prohibition on illegal or personal software installation, prohibition on unauthorised hardware alteration (except with the express written approval of the Head of IT), and the requirement to hand the laptop back in good condition upon leaving, transferring, or being directed by the company.

## Responsibility for Company Laptop
Every employee issued a company laptop is solely responsible for its upkeep and custody. If the laptop is damaged or lost due to the employee's negligence, Stemz Healthcare is entitled to recover from the employee all charges incurred for repairs or a new purchase.

## Use in Accordance With Company Policy
The laptop must be used in accordance with the policies of the company, in particular the "Acceptable Use of Internet" section of the IT Policy. Employees must ensure that the laptop is not used for any purpose that breaches company policies or the law of the land. The laptop must not be used for any illegal activity or gambling. Any illegal use of the laptop makes the employee solely responsible as an individual, not as an employee of Stemz Healthcare.

## No Unauthorised Software or Files
Employees must not install any illegal software, personal software, or unauthorised files (such as video, audio, or copyright-protected files) on the company laptop.

## No Hardware Alterations
Employees must not alter — or cause any alteration to — any hardware component of the laptop, except with express written approval from the Head of the IT department.

## Return of Laptop at Separation
Employees must hand over the laptop to Stemz Healthcare in good and working condition when they leave the company, are transferred, or are directed to return the laptop by the company.

## Employee Acknowledgement of Receipt
By signing the Laptop Acknowledgement Form, the employee acknowledges receipt of the laptop and agrees to the responsibilities and usage rules described above. A copy of the signed form is retained by the HR Department and the Accounts Department.
