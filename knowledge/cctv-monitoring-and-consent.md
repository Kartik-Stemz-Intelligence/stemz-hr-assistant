# CCTV Monitoring and Consent

## Overview
The Stemz CCTV Monitoring Policy governs workplace surveillance across Stemz office premises. It explains that Stemz Healthcare monitors its workplace and offices through CCTV cameras. At joining, every employee signs a CCTV Consent Form giving express consent to monitoring. The policy grants Stemz the right to store, process, and use CCTV data for legally permissible purposes (especially connected with the employee's employment), and clarifies that employees have no right of access or claim over the CCTV footage.

## Purpose of CCTV in the Workplace
Stemz Healthcare Pvt Ltd monitors its workplace and office premises through CCTV. The need for CCTV monitoring is explained to every employee at the time of joining.

## Employee Consent
By signing the CCTV Consent Form at joining, the employee expressly consents to being monitored through CCTV facilities placed within the office premises. Consent applies to CCTV monitoring as permitted under applicable law.

## Company Use of CCTV Data
The employee consents that Stemz Healthcare may store, process, or use the CCTV data for legally permissible purposes, especially in connection with the employee's employment with the company.

## No Employee Access to CCTV Data
Employees do not have any right of access to CCTV data. Employees cannot claim access to or ownership over the CCTV footage in any manner.
