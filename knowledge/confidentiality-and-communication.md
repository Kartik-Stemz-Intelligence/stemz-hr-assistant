# Confidentiality & Communication Undertaking

## Overview
The Stemz Confidentiality and Communication Undertaking is signed by every employee at joining. It prohibits any individual contact or communication unrelated to the employee's role with applicants, suppliers, or other parties who have a professional relationship with Stemz. It defines the consequences of divulging organisation information (immediate disciplinary action including possible termination and legal action), releases Stemz from liability for the employee's non-compliant activities, and formalises the employee's commitment to comply with Stemz policies and standard operating procedures.

## Prohibited Contact with External Parties
Employees are prohibited from having any individual contact or communication — not related to their role — with applicants, suppliers, or any individuals and organisations that have a professional relationship with Stemz Healthcare.

## Consequences of Divulging Information
If an employee is found to have divulged information related to the organisation, they will be subject to immediate disciplinary action. This may include termination of the employment contract and legal action.

## Release of Liability
The employee releases Stemz Healthcare from liability for any non-compliant activities they conduct in relation to contact or communication with applicants, suppliers, or individuals and organisations that have a professional relationship with Stemz Healthcare.

## Compliance with Policies
The employee confirms that they will comply with all Stemz Healthcare policies and standard operating procedures. Violation of these policies will result in disciplinary action.
