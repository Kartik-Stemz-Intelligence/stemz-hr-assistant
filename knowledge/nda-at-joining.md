# Non-Disclosure Agreement — Signed at Joining

## Overview
The Stemz Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) is a confidentiality agreement signed by every candidate at joining, before they are given access to any Stemz or Government information. It defines confidentiality obligations during and after employment, the requirement to return all materials on termination, the "work on hire" intellectual property clause (all IP developed by the employee while at Stemz belongs to Stemz), non-solicitation obligations, prohibitions on accepting or giving bribes, the possibility of transfer to any country for client requirements, and remedies available to Stemz for breach including injunctive relief.

## Purpose
This undertaking is signed by candidates selected and offered employment at Stemz before they are given access to any Stemz or Government information. It sets out the confidentiality obligations that apply during and after employment.

## Confidentiality Obligation
You must retain all Confidential Information in the strictest confidence. You must not disclose any confidential information to any person other than as necessary for your role at Stemz, and you must not use confidential information for any personal purpose.

## Continued Obligation After Termination
The obligation not to disclose or use Confidential Information continues even after termination of your employment with Stemz for any reason, unless you obtain prior written consent from the designated approving authority.

## Return of Materials on Termination
Upon termination of your employment, you must return to Stemz all materials, including all copies in any form, that contain Confidential Information and are in your possession or control.

## No Use of Confidential Information After Employment
You must not use or improperly disclose Confidential Information to others. This obligation remains in force until the confidential information becomes public without any breach of this undertaking by you.

## Intellectual Property — Work on Hire
Any intellectual property developed by you while employed with Stemz is deemed to be "work on hire". Proprietary rights on all such intellectual property lie exclusively with Stemz. You undertake to assign such rights to Stemz through a separate letter or assignment deed as prescribed by Stemz.

## No Bribes or Gifts
You must not accept or give any gifts, bribes, goods, or services to or from any government official or third party in order to facilitate any work completion.

## Deputation and Transfer
You may be deputed or transferred to any country depending on the requirements of Stemz under any contract with a new Embassy or client. You must comply with all applicable laws of the country where you are posted.

## Remedies for Breach
You acknowledge that monetary damages may not be an adequate remedy for a breach of your obligations. Stemz is entitled to seek relief by way of temporary injunction against you, in addition to any other remedies available under law, unless expressly waived in writing.

## Voluntary Execution
This undertaking is executed voluntarily by the employee, without any coercion or undue influence, after understanding its contents.
