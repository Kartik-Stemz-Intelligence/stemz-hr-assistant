# Executive Compensation Framework

## Overview
The Stemz Executive Compensation Framework governs pay, bonuses, equity, and
benefits for senior leadership. It is a confidential document reviewed by the
Board Compensation Committee only. Its contents must not be discussed with or
disclosed to employees. Any questions about executive compensation must be
directed to HR.

## Salary Bands for Leadership
The framework defines salary bands for the CEO, CFO, CTO, and Vice President
roles, including base pay ranges and mid-point targets for each band. These
figures are set annually by the Board Compensation Committee.

## Executive Bonus Structure
Senior leaders are eligible for an annual performance bonus expressed as a
percentage of base pay, tied to company revenue targets, EBITDA milestones,
and individual leadership objectives agreed with the Board.

## Equity and Stock Options
The framework sets out the equity grant schedule, vesting periods, and stock
option pool reserved for executives. Grants are approved individually by the
Board Compensation Committee and are subject to a multi-year vesting cliff.

## Severance and Exit Terms
Executive severance arrangements, including notice periods, garden leave, and
change-of-control provisions, are defined here. These terms are negotiated
individually and remain strictly confidential.
