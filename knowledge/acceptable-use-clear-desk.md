# Acceptable Use, Clear Desk, and Photography Rules

## Overview
The Stemz Acceptable Use, Clear Desk, and Photography Policy governs how employees use company systems and workspaces. Every Stemz employee undertakes to abide by the Acceptable Use Policy (governing systems, networks, and internet access), the Clear Desk and Clear Screen Policy (securing physical documents and locking screens when workstations are unattended), and a photography prohibition (no photographs are permitted inside the organisation using mobile phone cameras, still cameras, or video cameras, and photograph distribution is not allowed). Violations may result in disciplinary action under the organisation's existing disciplinary process.

## Scope
Every Stemz employee acknowledges and undertakes to abide by the Acceptable Use Policy and the Clear Desk and Clear Screen Policy at Stemz Healthcare.

## Acceptable Use Policy
Employees must use company systems, networks, and internet access in accordance with the Acceptable Use Policy. The full Acceptable Use Policy is a separate document maintained by IT; this undertaking is a written commitment to comply with it.

## Clear Desk and Clear Screen Policy
Employees must comply with the Clear Desk and Clear Screen Policy at all times. This includes securing physical documents and locking screens when workstations are unattended. The full policy is maintained by IT.

## Prohibition on Photography Inside the Organisation
Employees are prohibited from taking photographs inside the organisation using mobile phone cameras, still cameras, or video cameras. Taking photographs inside the organisation and distributing them is not permitted.

## Consequences of Violation
Violation of the Acceptable Use Policy, Clear Desk and Clear Screen Policy, or the photography prohibition can result in disciplinary action under the organisation's existing disciplinary process.
