# Stemz — Company Overview

## What Stemz Is
Stemz Global (operating as Stemz Healthcare) is a multinational healthcare group headquartered in the Netherlands. The company was founded in 2015 and delivers integrated medical services through an internally integrated system equipped with advanced technology. Stemz operates across the Middle East, Europe, and Southeast Asia.

## Mission
The company's mission is to help people live healthier and happier lives through leading-edge technologies and unparalleled care.

## Vision
The company's vision is to become one of the top 5 Healthcare brands in Asia through cutting-edge technology, delivering actionable and accessible diagnostics.

## Company Positioning
Stemz describes itself as "architects of wellness" — combining advanced technologies with compassion to create distinct healthcare experiences and to empower people toward improved health outcomes. The company positions itself as a thriving and innovative organisation with an unwavering pursuit of excellence and dedication to making a meaningful difference in people's lives.

## Business Verticals and Services
Stemz operates across nine business verticals:
- Visa Medical Services (immigration health examinations for governments and visa applicants; 15+ medical centres across 7 countries; more than 2 million tests annually with capacity for up to 5 million per year; the pioneer in Visa Medical outsourcing in the Middle East)
- Wellness Index Rating
- Cancer Care
- Medical Tourism
- Telemedicine
- Diagnostics (with advanced labs and AI-assisted diagnosis technology)
- Manufacturing (including cold-rolled formed sections for railways)
- IT Services
- Agri-Business (operations in Maharashtra and Northeast India)

## Team and Workforce
The Stemz team is composed of highly qualified healthcare professionals. Employees work across a collaborative, creative, and growth-oriented environment.

## Company Culture and Values
Stemz values collaboration, personal growth, and making a meaningful impact. The workplace culture emphasises:
- A collaborative, creative, and growth-oriented environment
- Professional development opportunities on cutting-edge projects
- Work-life balance as a company value
- A supportive work environment
- Recognition and rewards for talent
- Fostering creativity across teams

## Certifications and Accreditations
Stemz holds ISO 9001 (Quality Management) and ISO 27001 (Information Security) certifications globally, achieved in June 2025. The company holds CAP (College of American Pathologists) Accreditation for facilities in India, the Philippines, and Nepal. Stemz also holds NABL accreditation for its New Delhi facility.

## Awards and Recognition
Stemz Healthcare was celebrated as an Incredible Workplace in February 2026 — a recognition of workplace excellence and talent acquisition efforts. Stemz has been a Gold Partner at the Bharat Digital Healthcare Summit for two consecutive years (2024 and 2025).

## Quality Policy — Core Commitments
Quality is integral to all working practices at Stemz. The company's quality commitments include:
- Delivering efficient, high-quality services that meet or surpass client expectations, with regular performance monitoring and review
- Treating staff with dignity and respect while maintaining safe working conditions that comply with all legal and regulatory requirements
- Ensuring adequate resources, communication, training, and practical examples so quality is understood as an organisation-wide responsibility
- Running a comprehensive continual quality improvement programme with systematic review of procedures, action planning, and employee input
- Managing records and documentation per applicable regulations and using performance measurement data to inform improvement decisions
