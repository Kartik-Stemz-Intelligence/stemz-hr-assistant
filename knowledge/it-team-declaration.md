# IT Team Declaration

## Overview
The Stemz IT Team Declaration is a mandatory undertaking signed by IT team members only before they are granted system access. It covers five commitments: strict compliance with the IT approval matrix (breaches are gross misconduct), confidentiality of sensitive information (proprietary data, trade secrets, personal information), password security (no sharing under any circumstances), admin account discipline (no session-passing or unattended access), and the AI Tools Policy (no sensitive Stemz data — including source code and confidential information — may be uploaded to public generative AI tools such as ChatGPT). Non-compliance may result in disciplinary action, suspension, or termination of employment.

## Scope
This declaration applies to IT team members at Stemz Healthcare only. It is signed by IT staff before they are granted system access.

## IT Approval Matrix Compliance
IT team members commit to strict compliance with the company's mandatory IT approval matrix. Any activity undertaken through the IT team member's designated ID without obtaining the necessary approvals is considered a breach of compliance. Such breaches may fall into the category of gross misconduct and may lead to disciplinary action.

## Confidentiality Obligation
IT team members recognise the importance of maintaining confidentiality of sensitive information entrusted to them during employment. This obligation covers all forms of information, including proprietary data, trade secrets, personal information of clients or employees, and other confidential material. Unauthorised access, use, or disclosure is prohibited. Breaches of confidentiality may result in legal action and damage to the reputation and trust of the organisation.

## Password Security
IT team members must not share any of their personal or admin passwords with any third party or other team member, under any circumstances. Maintaining password confidentiality is critical to organisational security.

## Admin Account Discipline
IT team members must not log into any system with an admin account and then pass the session to another user or leave it unattended. Such actions expose the organisation to security vulnerabilities, including security breaches, and are prohibited.

## AI Tools Policy
No sensitive, official, confidential information, or source code owned by Stemz may be uploaded, shared, or processed through any public or external AI tools. Company data remains the exclusive property of Stemz and must be kept confidential and secure. This restriction applies to all public generative AI tools (such as ChatGPT and similar).

## Consequences of Non-Compliance
Non-compliance with the above terms may result in serious consequences, including disciplinary action, suspension, or termination of employment.
