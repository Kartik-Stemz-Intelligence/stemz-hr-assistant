# Stemz — History and Milestones

## Founding
Stemz was founded in 2015 as a multinational healthcare group headquartered in the Netherlands. The company's operational timeline of documented healthcare services begins in 2018.

## 2018 to 2019 — Government Immigration Services
Stemz established visa medical centres across South and Southeast Asia, expanding to 14 facilities. Coverage included Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, and cities in Bangladesh, Nepal, and the Philippines. In 2019, Stemz launched Qatar visa centres in India, Nepal, and Bangladesh.

## 2020 — COVID-19 Pivot
Stemz pivoted to COVID-19 testing services during the pandemic, emerging as the largest aggregator for COVID-19 services. This period also marked Stemz's entry into the B2C market with global operations.

## 2021 — Telemedicine Launch and FIFA World Cup Qatar
Stemz partnered with more than 60 international clients for COVID testing. The company launched telemedicine services in 2021 and provided healthcare services during the FIFA World Cup in Qatar.

## 2022 — Diagnostics, Agri-Business, Manufacturing
Stemz introduced diagnostic services with advanced labs and launched the Wellness Index Rating product. The company expanded into agri-business in Maharashtra. In 2022, Stemz secured CAP and NABL accreditations, launched radiology services, and began manufacturing cold-rolled formed sections for railways.

## 2023 — Global Diagnostic Expansion and AI Adoption
Stemz achieved CAP accreditation for facilities in India, the Philippines, and Nepal in 2023. Diagnostic services expanded to more than 5 countries, and the company implemented AI-assisted diagnosis technology. Agri-business operations extended to Northeast India. In October 2023, Stemz announced a strategic collaboration with Microbe&lab.

## 2024 — Saudi Arabia Joint Venture
In July 2024, Stemz Global established a joint venture with AlSuwaiket Group, a major Saudi Arabian conglomerate, to advance healthcare projects in the region. In April 2024, Stemz was Gold Partner at the Bharat Digital Healthcare Summit 2024.

## 2025 — ISO Certifications, DeepTek Partnership, CAP Reaffirmed
In February 2025, Stemz Global announced a strategic partnership with DeepTek focused on AI-driven healthcare transformation. In May 2025, Stemz served as Gold Partner at the Bharat Digital Healthcare Summit 2025 for the second consecutive year. In June 2025, Stemz Group achieved ISO 9001 (Quality Management) and ISO 27001 (Information Security) certifications globally. In August 2025, Stemz reaffirmed its CAP Accreditation for facilities in India and the Philippines.

## 2026 — Incredible Workplace Recognition and Bio-Bitumen Technology
On February 6, 2026, Stemz Healthcare was celebrated as an Incredible Workplace, recognising workplace excellence and talent acquisition efforts. On February 2, 2026, Negam Care — a Stemz group company — secured a licence for CSIR-developed technology to produce bio-bitumen from agricultural waste, marking a partnership in sustainable technology.

## Key Certifications Held Currently
The company holds ISO 9001, ISO 27001, and CAP Accreditations. NABL accreditation is held for the New Delhi diagnostic facility.

## Awards
Stemz Healthcare was recognised as an Incredible Workplace in February 2026. Stemz has been a Gold Partner at the Bharat Digital Healthcare Summit for two consecutive years (2024 and 2025).

## Major Partnerships
Stemz maintains active partnerships and joint ventures with AlSuwaiket Group (Saudi Arabia, 2024), DeepTek (AI-driven healthcare, 2025), and Microbe&lab (2023). Stemz has partnered with more than 60 international clients for COVID testing services.
