# Employee Declaration at Exit

## Overview
The Stemz Employee Declaration at Exit is signed by every separating employee. It reaffirms the confidentiality obligations from the Non-Disclosure Agreement signed at joining, extending them for 5 years post-employment. The employee confirms they have handed over all confidential information (files, data, records, reports) to the designated handover recipients per the handover form, that they have not retained confidential information in any form (including deleting all electronic copies from personal devices), and that they will notify the CEO immediately of any breach of these obligations or conflict of interest. The signed declaration is retained by HR in the separation file. Breach may result in disciplinary or legal action.

## Reference to Non-Disclosure Agreement
Every separating employee acknowledges the Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) executed with Stemz Healthcare at the time of joining. The Employee Declaration reaffirms the confidentiality obligations under that NDA at the point of separation.

## 5-Year Post-Employment Confidentiality
The employee affirms that they will not disclose to any other person, firm, or corporation any confidential information relating to ongoing or past projects of the company. This obligation continues for a period of 5 years from the end of the employee's employment with Stemz.

## Handover of Confidential Information
The employee confirms that they have handed over all Confidential Information which they had access to during their tenure. This includes files, data, records, reports, schedules, and other business-related information — whether in electronic, printable, or any other form — to the designated handover recipients as per the handover form.

## No Retention on Personal Devices
The employee confirms that they have not retained the Confidential Information in any form. All electronic Confidential Information has been deleted from the employee's personal devices.

## Notification of Breach
The employee understands that they must notify the CEO of the company immediately of any breach of their obligations, or any conflict of interest under this declaration, that comes to their attention.

## Retention of the Declaration
The signed Employee Declaration is retained by HR in the employee's separation file. The CEO is notified only in the event of a clause-3 breach.

## Consequences of Breach
Any breach of this declaration may result in irreparable damage to the company. It may lead to disciplinary action or legal action against the departing employee.

## Confirmation of Understanding
By signing and returning a copy of this declaration to the CEO of the company, the employee confirms their understanding and acceptance of the above clauses and their commitment to comply.
