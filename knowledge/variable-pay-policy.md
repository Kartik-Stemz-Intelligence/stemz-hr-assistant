# Variable Pay Policy

## Overview
The Stemz Variable Pay Policy governs performance-linked variable compensation for employees. It defines the objective (linking rewards to performance), scope (all full-time and part-time employees; consultants excluded), eligibility rules (probation confirmed, no active disciplinary record, notice-period disqualification, treatment of long leaves), the variable payment grid based on OKR rating and KRA achievement, evaluation components (70% individual performance, 20% 360-degree feedback, 10% business impact), bi-annual payout timing (before 31 August for Jan-Jun cycle, before 28/29 February for Jul-Dec cycle), management discretion, recovery of erroneous payments, and mandatory HRBP notification duties around variable-pay disqualification.

## Objective
The objective of this policy is to establish a fair and transparent framework that directly links employee rewards to individual, team, and organisational performance. The policy motivates employees, drives achievement of business goals, and recognises exceptional contributions through performance-based monetary incentives.

## Scope
This policy covers all full-time and part-time employees of Stemz. Consultants, interns, contractors, and other non-payroll staff are excluded from variable pay unless an exception approval is obtained from executive management and is clearly documented in their contracts or agreements.

## Eligibility — Probation Requirement
An employee's probation status must be confirmed as of the cut-off date for finalising performance-linked variable pay eligibility. Employees still on probation on the cut-off date are not eligible for variable pay for that cycle.

## Eligibility — Notice Period Disqualification
Employees who have resigned and are serving notice before the payout date are disqualified from receiving performance-linked variable pay for that cycle. Management-discretion exceptions may be granted only for senior critical roles, and only when justified in writing and approved by the CEO or COO.

## Eligibility — Long Leave
Employees on sabbatical or long leaves such as maternity leave are not eligible for performance-linked variable pay during the leave. However, such employees become eligible again after resuming their official duties. Employees on approved long-term leave may be eligible for pro-rata payouts on return, subject to management approval.

## Eligibility — Disciplinary Record
Employees with any major disciplinary record, warning letters, or compliance breaches during the assessment cycle are not eligible for variable pay, either completely or partially, depending on the nature of the breach.

## Variable Payment Grid Based on Performance Rating
The variable pay release is directly tied to the OKR rating and KRA achievement. An OKR rating of 4.75 to 5.00 with 100 to 120 percent KRA achievement releases 100 to 120 percent of variable pay and places the employee as Exceptional Talent. An OKR rating of 4.50 to 4.75 with 100 percent KRA achievement releases 100 percent of variable pay under the Exceeds Expectations band. An OKR rating of 4.25 to 4.50 with 90 to 100 percent KRA achievement releases 90 to 100 percent of variable pay under Exceeds Expectations. An OKR rating of 4.00 to 4.25 with 80 to 90 percent KRA achievement releases 80 to 90 percent of variable pay under Meets Expectations. An OKR rating of 3.75 to 4.00 with 75 to 80 percent KRA achievement releases 70 to 80 percent of variable pay under Meets Expectations. An OKR rating of 3.50 to 3.75 with 70 to 75 percent KRA achievement releases 60 to 70 percent of variable pay under Meets Expectations. An OKR rating of 3.25 to 3.50 with 65 to 70 percent KRA achievement releases 50 to 60 percent of variable pay under Needs Improvement. An OKR rating of 3.00 to 3.25 with 60 to 65 percent KRA achievement releases 40 to 50 percent of variable pay under Needs Improvement. An OKR rating of 2.50 to 3.00 with 50 to 60 percent KRA achievement releases 0 to 40 percent of variable pay under Non-Performers. An OKR rating below 2.50 with less than 50 percent KRA achievement releases 0 percent variable pay under Non-Performers.

## Clear Targets and KRAs
All employees must have clear, documented KRAs or goals set at the beginning of each appraisal cycle. Any exceptions must be justified in writing by the manager and approved by HR. In cases where goals were not set or were revised, eligibility will be determined based on manager justification and HR approval.

## Components of Variable Pay Evaluation
Variable pay evaluation is weighted across three components. Individual Performance contributes 70 percent of the score. 360-degree feedback from peers, other managers, external stakeholders, and subordinates contributes 20 percent. The department's or individual's impact on business contributes 10 percent.

## Payout Frequency
Variable pay is paid bi-annually within 2 months of completing the performance review cycle. For the Performance Review Cycle of January to June, the payout happens before 31 August. For the Performance Review Cycle of July to December, the payout happens before 28 or 29 February.

## Exceptions
Any variable pay exceptions — including for consultants, employees moving to consultant status, or payouts during notice period — require documented exception approval from Management (CEO or COO). All exception approvals must be filed with HR for audit compliance. Exceptions are granted only in rare, business-critical cases.

## Management Discretion
The management reserves the right to hold, defer, or withdraw variable pay at any time and without prior notice, under circumstances including exceptional business situations, regulatory requirements, financial exigencies, compliance breaches, or other conditions deemed critical by Leadership. Any decision to hold or withdraw variable pay is made at the discretion of the CEO or a designated leadership panel and is documented for audit and compliance purposes.

## Recovery of Excess or Erroneous Variable Pay
If any excess or erroneous payment of variable pay is made due to administrative, procedural, or calculation error, management retains the right to recover the excess amount from the affected employee. Recovery is executed with reasonable due notice, outlining the amount, reason for recovery, and process for repayment. The employee retains the right to request clarification or raise concerns through HR within 7 to 14 days of notice. All recovery actions comply with local labour laws applicable in the employee's location.

## HRBP Notification Responsibility
As part of this policy, the HRBP team must ensure that all employees are informed at the time of joining about the company's stance on variable pay eligibility and retention-based payments. Specifically, any employee who resigns and is serving notice during the variable pay assessment period is ineligible for such payments for that cycle, unless an explicit management exception exists and is documented. Written acknowledgement is obtained from the employee during onboarding and maintained in personnel records. At the time of resignation, the HRBP team must reiterate this notification in writing and record it in the employee's separation documentation before the final settlement process.

## Communication and Governance
Eligible employees receive a variable pay letter with the final amount. The policy is reviewed annually by HR and management. The final decision on payout lies with the Leadership and HR team, after approval by CEO or COO.
