# Employee Self-Declaration for Lost or Damaged IT Assets

## Overview
The Stemz Employee Self-Declaration for Lost or Damaged IT Assets is submitted by any employee whose issued IT asset is lost or damaged, either during employment or at the time of separation. Covered assets include Laptop, Laptop Adaptor, Laptop Bag, Mouse, Wi-Fi Dongle, Headphone, Laptop Stand, Screen Guard, and Mobile Handset. By signing, the employee acknowledges their duty of care over company assets, the company's ownership of all issued assets, the company's right to recover the cost of lost or damaged assets from the Full and Final settlement, and authorises Stemz to recover repair/maintenance/replacement costs from the employee's payables. The declaration is signed by the employee, the HR SPOC, and the IT SPOC.

## When This Form Applies
This self-declaration is submitted by the employee when any IT asset assigned to them is lost or damaged. It applies both to currently employed staff and to employees at the time of separation from the company.

## Asset Types Covered
The self-declaration covers a range of IT assets, including Laptop, Laptop Adaptor, Laptop Bag, Mouse, Wi-Fi Dongle, Headphone, Laptop Stand, Screen Guard, and Mobile Handset.

## Employee Duty of Care for Assets
The employee acknowledges that they were informed in advance of their duty to take the same amount of care of the assets as if they were the employee's own. The employee was also informed of the duty to return the assets in the same physical and working condition as when they were provided by Stemz, at the time of relieving.

## Assets Remain Company Property
The employee acknowledges that the assets assigned to them during their tenure are the property of the company. The assets were provided to enable the employee to deliver their duties.

## Recovery from Full and Final Settlement
The employee acknowledges that the company reserves the right to recover the cost of lost or damaged IT assets from the employee's full and final (F&F) settlement.

## Authorisation for Recovery of Repair or Replacement Costs
The employee undertakes to allow Stemz to recover from their payables — in any manner Stemz deems fit — the cost of repair, maintenance, and replacement of damaged, lost, or destructed assets (whether physically or operationally damaged).

## Details Provided in the Declaration
For each lost or damaged asset, the employee records the asset name, model or serial number, and approximate cost. The employee also records the date on which the asset was lost.

## Signatures Required
The Self-Declaration is signed by the employee, the HR Single Point of Contact (SPOC), and the IT SPOC. Each records their name, signature, and date.
