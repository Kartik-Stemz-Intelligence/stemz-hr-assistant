# Anti-Bribery and Non-Solicitation Undertaking

## Overview
The Stemz Anti-Bribery and Non-Solicitation Undertaking is signed by every employee at joining. It confirms the employee's awareness of Stemz policies on anti-bribery and non-solicitation activities, which apply to interactions with applicants, suppliers, and any individuals or organisations with a professional relationship to Stemz. It outlines consequences of violations (immediate disciplinary action including possible termination and legal action) and releases Stemz from liability for the employee's non-compliant activities in relation to bribery or solicitation.

## Awareness of Policies
The employee confirms that they understand and are aware of all Stemz Healthcare policies relating to anti-bribery and non-solicitation activities. These policies apply to interactions with applicants, suppliers, and any individuals or organisations that have a professional relationship with Stemz Healthcare.

## Consequences of Violation
The employee acknowledges that a violation of Stemz Healthcare anti-bribery or non-solicitation policies will result in immediate disciplinary action. This includes possible termination of the employment contract and legal action against the employee.

## Release of Liability
The employee releases Stemz Healthcare from liability for any non-compliant activities in relation to bribery or solicitation with applicants, suppliers, or individuals and organisations that have a professional relationship with Stemz Healthcare.

## Compliance Obligation
The employee undertakes to comply with all Stemz Healthcare policies and standard operating procedures in their role. Violation will result in disciplinary action.
