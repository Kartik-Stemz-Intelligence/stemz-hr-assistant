# Attendance Policy

## Overview
The Stemz Attendance Policy defines how employee attendance, work hours, and time-off are tracked and enforced at Stemz. It covers standard office timings (9:00 AM to 6:00 PM with a required 9 daily gross hours and 45 weekly hours), late-arrival rules with a 10-minute grace period and 3 tolerated incidents per month, weekly and daily work-hour shortage penalties, missing swipes, biometric time tracking, work-from-home (1 day per month with 2 days prior notice), On-Duty (OD) requests for full-day / half-day / hourly work outside office, and regularization requests to correct attendance discrepancies (up to 3 per month). Penalties for breaches are typically 0.5 day of Paid Leave, with Unpaid Leave deducted if no Paid Leave is available.

## Office Timings and Daily Work Quota
The standard office timings at Stemz are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Every employee is required to work a total of 9 hours per day (gross hours). There is a flexibility exception: an employee may start work as late as 10:00 AM, but the 9-hour daily quota still applies, so a 10:00 AM start requires working until 7:00 PM to complete 9 hours.

## No Attendance
You are not penalized for any leave if no attendance is logged by you.

## Late Arrival
There is a grace period (tolerance) of 10 minutes for arrival. Under the standard 9:00 AM start, this means arrival by 9:10 AM is not considered late. If you are using the flexible start-time exception (starting work up to 10:00 AM), the grace period applies to that start time — so arrival by 10:10 AM at the latest is not considered late.

You may arrive late up to 3 times in a month with no penalty. Beyond 3 late arrivals in a month, a penalty of 0.5 day of Paid Leave applies for every 1 additional late incident. You have a buffer period of 2 days to regularize your attendance before the penalty is applied. If no Paid Leave is available, Unpaid Leave is deducted instead.

## Work Hours - Weekly Shortage
The strict weekly work quota is 45 hours from Monday to Friday (5 days × 9 hours). If your average gross hours in a week are less than 100% of shift hours — that is, less than 45 hours — a penalty of 0.5 day of Paid Leave applies.

## Work Hours - Daily Shortage
The daily minimum work quota is 90% of the 9-hour shift, which is 8 hours 6 minutes (8.1 hours). It is not mandatory to complete the strict 9-hour quota every single day, but each day must meet the 90% floor. If your average gross hours in a day are less than 90% of shift hours (less than 8 hours 6 minutes), a penalty of 0.5 day of Paid Leave applies. You have a buffer period of 2 days to regularize your attendance before the penalty is applied. If no Paid Leave is available, Unpaid Leave is deducted instead. Note that even if daily shortage does not apply, the weekly 45-hour quota must still be met.

## Missing Swipes
There is no penalty for missing swipes. You are still recommended to regularize your attendance.

## Time Tracking Method
Your attendance is automatically tracked using biometric devices and web clock-in.

## Work From Home (WFH)
You are allowed 1 day of WFH per month, and only as a full day (half-day WFH is not permitted).
You are required to clock in and out during WFH. Late clock-in, no clock-in, or insufficient gross hours will be penalized under the penalization policy.
A WFH request requires 2 days of prior notice.
All WFH requests require approval.

## On-Duty (OD)
You can raise an OD request for a full day, a half day, or on an hourly basis.
You are required to clock in and out during OD. Late clock-in, no clock-in, or insufficient gross hours will be penalized under the penalization policy.
You can raise an OD request for past dates within the current month, until the last day of the current month.
All OD requests require approval.

## Regularization
If you are penalized due to an attendance discrepancy, you can request regularization up to 3 times per month.
All regularization requests require approval.
