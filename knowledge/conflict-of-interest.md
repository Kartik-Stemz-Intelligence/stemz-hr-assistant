# Conflict of Interest Declaration

## Overview
The Stemz Conflict of Interest Policy defines and manages conflicts arising from employee or immediate-family (parents, spouse, children) business interests that overlap with Stemz's business. Employees complete this declaration at joining. The declaration has three sections: Section A (no conflict), Section B (positive declaration of existing outside interests including role, remuneration, and time commitment), and Section C (relatives working within Stemz Healthcare, including any direct reporting relationships). It imposes an ongoing obligation to declare any future conflicts within 24 hours of becoming aware, in writing, to both the Reporting Manager and the HR function.

## What Counts as a Conflict of Interest
A conflict of interest exists when the employee or their immediate family (parents, spouse, or children) has any business interest, association, or stake in an entity that benefits from the employee's employment with Stemz Healthcare. It also exists when the employee or immediate family has interests that compete — directly or indirectly, wholly or partly — with Stemz's business of providing healthcare services for diplomatic missions and governments, or with any business conducted by Stemz. Any direct or indirect business activity by the employee or immediate family that is similar to or overlaps with the employee's role at Stemz Healthcare is also a conflict of interest.

## Immediate Family Definition
For this policy, immediate family means the employee's parents, spouse, and children.

## Section A — No Conflict Declaration
If the employee has no outside business interests that would constitute a conflict of interest, they complete Section A of the Conflict of Interest Declaration form, confirming that neither they nor their immediate family have any such interests.

## Section B — Declaring a Conflict
If the employee has a positive declaration to make — meaning they do have a potential conflict of interest — they must complete Section B of the form. Section B requires details of the outside interest, including the name and type of organisation, role title, business activity, time commitment during working hours, and any remuneration received. Section B is submitted to the HR function with a copy to the reporting manager for guidance.

## Section C — Relatives Working at Stemz
If the employee has relatives working within Stemz Healthcare, they must complete Section C by listing the names and relationships. If there is any direct reporting relationship with any of these relatives, the employee must specify the details.

## Ongoing Obligation to Declare Future Conflicts
If any situation arises in the future that may result in — or potentially result in — an actual or potential conflict of interest, the employee must declare it immediately in writing to their Reporting Manager and the HR function. The declaration must be made prior to engaging in the business activity, and in any event within 24 hours of the employee becoming aware of the situation. Full details of the situation must be provided.
