# Change of Circumstances Notification

## Overview
The Stemz Change of Circumstances Policy requires employees to notify HR of any change to their personal circumstances or their dependants' circumstances during employment. At joining, employees declare they have not been involved in espionage, terrorism, sabotage, or actions to overthrow parliamentary democracy. Ongoing notification is required for: name changes (with evidence), nationality changes (with new passport or certificate), new passport issuance (with details of number/issue/expiry, and police report if the previous was lost or stolen), criminal convictions (with court, sentencing, and offence details), address and contact-information changes, and dependant status changes (relationship breakdowns, new children).

## Background Declaration at Joining
At joining, employees declare that they have never been involved in — nor been associated with — espionage, terrorism, sabotage, or actions intended to overthrow or undermine parliamentary democracy by political, industrial, or violent means. They also declare that they are not, and have not recently been, a member of any organisation which has advocated such activities, or been associated with such an organisation or its members in a way that raises reasonable doubts about their reliability. Employees confirm that they are not susceptible to pressure or improper influence, and have not shown dishonesty or lack of integrity that throws doubt on their reliability.

## Ongoing Notification Obligation
Every employee must notify the HR function of any change in circumstances — regarding themselves or their dependants — that affects the categories listed below. Notification must be made through HR.

## Name Change Notification
If the employee's name changes (for example due to marriage), they must provide their current full name and title, along with evidence such as a marriage certificate or deed poll.

## Nationality Change Notification
If the employee's nationality changes at any point during employment with Stemz, they must clearly state their previous and current nationality. Evidence such as a new passport or a certificate of nationality issued by the government of the country is required.

## New Passport Notification
If a new passport is issued, the employee must provide the passport number, place of issue, date of issue, and date of expiry. If the new passport was issued because the original was lost or stolen, the employee must enclose the crime reference number or police report from when the loss or theft was reported.

## Criminal Conviction Notification
If the employee is convicted of any offence at any point during their employment with Stemz, they must provide full details of the offence, the name of the court, the date and place of sentencing, and the sentence imposed.

## Address Change Notification
If the employee changes address, they must provide both the previous and new address in full, including the postcode.

## Contact Information Changes
If the employee's telephone number changes, they must provide the full number including the area code. If the email address changes, they must state the amended email address.

## Dependants Notification
Dependants who cease to be dependants for any reason must be notified to HR. If a dependant partner relationship has permanently broken down, the employee must provide the date they ceased living together and, if possible, the partner's new address. Information about any new-born child of the employee must be provided.

## General Notification
Employees may use the same notification process to inform HR of any other information they believe the company should know.
