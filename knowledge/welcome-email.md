# Welcome Email — Pre-joining Guide

## Office Location
The Head Office is at Plot No. 31, Udyog Vihar, Phase 1, Gurugram, Haryana - 122022. A QR code for directions is shared in the welcome email attachment.

## Documents to Bring on Day 1
You are required to bring hard-copy printouts of the following documents on your first day. Bring 2 copies each of Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Passport (if available), and Passport-size photos. Bring 1 copy each of 10th (High School) marksheet and pass certificate, 12th (Intermediate) marksheet and pass certificate, Graduation marksheet and certificate, Post-Graduation marksheet and certificate, and any other education certificates. From your previous employment, bring 1 copy each of the Appointment Letter, Relieving Letter or Resignation Acceptance, Salary Slips for the last 3 months, Latest Increment Letter, and Experience Letters from all organisations you have worked with.

## What to Expect on Day 1
Your first day will include an induction session where the HR team answers questions about the company, policies, and your role. You will be introduced to your team. You will also receive your laptop, official Stemz email ID, and company welcome merchandise (swag kit) as part of Day 1.

## Lunch on Day 1
Lunch on your first day is provided by the company at no cost to you.
