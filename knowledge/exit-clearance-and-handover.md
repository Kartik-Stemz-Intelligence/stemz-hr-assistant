# Exit Clearance and Handover Form

## Overview
The Stemz Exit Clearance and Handover Policy governs the process every leaving employee must complete before their Full and Final settlement is paid. It requires a fully signed Exit Clearance and Handover Form that captures employee particulars, type of separation (resignation/termination/redundancy/absconding/transfer), notice period status, email and system ID deletion timelines, and handover status from the line manager. Clearance items are collected across four departments: IT (physical assets and access rights), HR and Line Manager (statutory cards, insurance, ID cards, Keka exit survey), Finance (petty cash, LTA, credit cards, salary advances, reimbursements), and Legal (contracts, NDAs, regulatory tracker). The employee formally confirms handover of all files and ongoing confidentiality obligations. Signatures are required from the employee, line manager, and Country HR.

## Purpose
The Exit Clearance and Handover Form is completed by every leaving employee at exit. It ensures sign-off by appropriate department representatives on all clearance items. The final settlement is not paid until the form is completed and returned to HR.

## Employee Details Recorded
The form records the employee's name, employee code, designation, department, reporting manager, head of department, date of joining, date of resignation, last working day (LWD) as per notice, actual last working day, location, personal email ID, personal contact number, and permanent address.

## Type of Separation
The form captures the type of separation — resignation, termination or redundancy, absconding, or transfer. It also captures the status of the notice period — completed, not completed, waived, or subject to recovery — and the number of notice days.

## Email and System ID Deletion
The form specifies when the employee's Email, TMS, Helpdesk, Glosys, and Domain ID should be deleted. The deletion timeline can be immediate, after 30 days, or after 60 days from the last working day. If emails are to be forwarded to a line manager or successor, the recipient's name and email ID are recorded.

## Handover Requirements from Line Manager
The line manager records the handover status of documentation, filing, contacts, systems access, training, tasks, projects, and work in progress. The line manager also records any comments on the handover.

## IT Clearance Items
IT clearance covers physical assets and access rights. The physical assets recovered include laptop and laptop bag or desktop, IT liability items (CDs, DVDs, hard disk drives, mouse, dongle, USB storage), uniform (if applicable), cabinet, drawer and locker keys, medical insurance cards, stationery and letterheads, business cards (if applicable), company documents, data and stamps, employee photo ID card with card holder and lanyard, building access card and parking card, data card, SIM card or mobile phone, and access card to office or building. Access-related items include deletion of access to shared folders, applications, domain, email ID, and internal software.

## HR and Line Manager Clearance Items
HR and Line Manager clearance items include labour cards and statutory cards (EPF, SSS, PhilHealth, HDMF, ESIC if applicable), medical insurance cards, employee ID card, exit interview via the Keka exit survey, and informing the bank of the employee leaving.

## Finance Clearance Items
Finance clearance items include petty cash and LTA, company credit card, salary advance, reimbursements (if any), and any other pending financial items.

## Legal Clearance Items
Legal clearance items include contracts, non-disclosure agreements, and any other confidential information (as applicable). Legal also collects the Regulatory Tracker, which highlights all key details including existing licenses and their expiry information.

## Handover of Files and Records
The separating employee confirms in writing that they have handed over necessary files and records with full explanation of the documentation. The name, designation, and signature of the person receiving the handover are recorded. The separating employee also confirms that duties, responsibilities, and pending actions have been explained to and understood by the employee taking over. Contact details of any third parties the separating employee was dealing with are also formally handed over.

## Employee Confirmation and Ongoing Confidentiality
The separating employee confirms that they have handed over all information, documents, products, and items belonging to the company. The employee understands that the company reserves the right to request the return of — or payment for — any property, or the settlement of any outstanding obligations, that might have been excluded from the clearance process. On separation, the employee acknowledges an ongoing responsibility to maintain the confidentiality of all information they may have been aware of during their employment with Stemz.

## Sign-Off Requirements
The form requires signatures from the employee, the line manager, and Country HR, each with a date. Any items retained by the employee (such as a medical card) are listed separately, and the incomplete form is handed to HR on the last working day. The retained items are then tracked and signed off when appropriately returned.
